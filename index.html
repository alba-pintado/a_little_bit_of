by Alba Pintado Rodrigo

Hi! I’m Alba Pintado Rodrigo, a biologist and bioinformatician from Madrid, Spain.

I enjoy staying active, following a healthy lifestyle, and exploring new ways to make science accessible and engaging for curious minds.

Through this platform, I aim to combine rigorous scientific knowledge with practical insights in bioinformatics, education, and everyday life.

Hi there, and welcome!

I’m really glad you’re here.

This space was born out of curiosity and a passion for sharing knowledge in an authentic way, blending personal and professional experiences.

Here, you’ll find insights, discoveries, and tools that can inspire you, guide you, and help you learn a little more every day.

My goal is to create a place where you feel comfortable exploring ideas, asking questions, and discovering new perspectives—both in my professional work and in the small everyday adventures that teach us so much.

Thank you for stopping by. I’m excited to share this journey with you, and I hope you enjoy reading and learning as much as I enjoy creating this content.
