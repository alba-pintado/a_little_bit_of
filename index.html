Hi there, and welcome!

I’m Alba Pintado Rodrigo, a biologist and bioinformatician from Madrid, Spain.

I enjoy staying active, following a healthy lifestyle, and exploring new ways to make science accessible and engaging for curious minds.

This space was born out of curiosity and a passion for sharing knowledge in an authentic way, blending personal and professional experiences. Here you’ll find insights, discoveries and tools that can inspire you, guide you, and help you learn a little more every day.

My goal is to create a place where you feel comfortable exploring ideas, asking questions, and discovering new perspectives, both in my professional work and in the small everyday adventures that teach us so much.

Thank you for stopping by. I hope you enjoy reading and learning as much as I enjoy creating this content.

— Alba
