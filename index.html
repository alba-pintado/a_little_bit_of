A Little Bit Of…

For the curious and weird ones

by Alba Pintado Rodrigo

Hi! I’m Alba Pintado Rodrigo, a biologist and bioinformatician from Madrid, Spain.

I enjoy staying active, following a healthy lifestyle, and exploring new ways to make science accessible and engaging for curious minds.

Through this platform, I aim to combine rigorous scientific knowledge with practical insights in bioinformatics, education, and everyday life.

The aim of this vlog is to share my passions professionally and clearly. I cover:

  • Science & Bioinformatics: explaining concepts, analyses, and discoveries
  • Lifestyle: tips for healthy routines and active living
  • Books, Movies & Music: personal recommendations and reviews
  • Travel: cultural and natural explorations
  • Tutoring: educational resources for students

Everything is designed to reach curious minds worldwide, inspire learning, and spark curiosity.
